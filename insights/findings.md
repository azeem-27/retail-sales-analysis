# Key Findings

## 1. Overall Sales Performance
Total revenue was £456,000 across a 1,000 transactions, with 2,514 units sold. Average transaction value was £456.

This provides the baseline for comparing performance across categories and customer groups.

## 2. Product Category
The analysis shows that Electronics generated the highest revenue, contributing £156,905 to total sales in 2023.

However, Clothing saw a higher number of units sold and transactions. This suggests that revenue wasn't distributed the same way as sales volume.

A possible explanation could be that Electronics tend to be expensive and cost more per unit compared to the other categories.

## 3. Sales Over Time
Monthly revenue varied throughout the year, with the highest revenue occurring in May and the lowest in September. The chart follows a stationary pattern with regular fluctuations.

However, the dataset does not contain information about season based marketing or other external factors. So the reason for the changes cannot be determined from the available data.

## 4. Customer Demographics
Female customers accounted for the larger share across revenue, transactions and units sold. Male customers however, were just short of.

The 36-50 group generated the highest revenue, while 18-25 group had the highest average transaction value.

This reveals a correlation between age and average transaction value, where younger people tend to spend more per transaction.

However, demographics data does not necessarily indicate which group is the most valuable customer segment. It requires further analysis using metrics like purchase frequency or lifetime value to provide a better understanding of customer value.

# Questions for Further Investigation
- Which product categories generate the highest profit rather than revenue?
- What factors contributed to the peaks and declines in monthly revenue?
- Do particular customer demographics show stronger preferences for specific product categories?
- How would discounts and promotions affect sales performance?
- Which products or customers contribute most to overall revenue?

# Limitations
The analysis is based on transaction sales data and because of that it focuses primarily on revenue and sales volume. It does not include costs, profit margins, discounts, marketing activity, inventory or customer motivations.

As a result, the findings identify patterns and relationships in the sales data but cannot establish profitability or the causes behind observed trends.
